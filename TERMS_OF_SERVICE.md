## 1. Scope of Hosted Services
The hosted signaling server provides matchmaking, peer discovery, and fallback encrypted relaying for user-space P2P sessions. The software itself is open source under AGPLv3; these terms govern access to the **hosted cloud service infrastructure**.

You agree to use Rakhssh hosted services exclusively for lawful administrative, educational, and development purposes:
1. **Authorized Access Only:** You may only establish connections to hosts, machines, and networks that you own or have explicit authorization to administer.
2. **Prohibited Conduct:**
